CoworkingView Price Index CronJob {{ include "cwv.fullname" . }} is installed.

It runs `cwv {{ .Values.command }}` from {{ .Values.image.repository }}:{{ .Values.image.tag }}
on schedule {{ .Values.schedule | quote }}. Output is the job log. JSON includes the
attribution string; table and CSV print it on stderr.

Price Index data is CC BY 4.0. Credit CoworkingView Price Index and keep the
attribution the CLI prints with any figure you republish.
https://coworkingview.com/en/tools/price-index
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The chart and the cwv binary are MIT. The image is FROM scratch and runs as uid 65534.

  kubectl get cronjob -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}
  kubectl create job --from=cronjob/{{ include "cwv.fullname" . | trunc 52 | trimSuffix "-" }} {{ include "cwv.fullname" . | trunc 52 | trimSuffix "-" }}-once
